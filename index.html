About

I am a Senior Data Engineer with over 5 years of experience architecting and implementing scalable cloud data solutions across AWS, Azure, and GCP. I have a proven track record of delivering measurable business outcomes — reducing operational costs by 20–67% while improving system performance through modern data infrastructure.

My expertise spans enterprise-grade ETL/ELT pipelines, real-time streaming architectures, data warehouse design, and BI reporting layers. I hold a DP-203 Azure Data Engineering certification and a BSc in Computer Science from Ajayi Crowther University.

Beyond my professional work, I teach data analysis skills for free to NYSC corps members across Lagos State, helping young Nigerians break into the technology industry. I also build open source tools at the intersection of AI and community impact.
